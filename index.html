Privacy Policy & Data Use

Last updated: November 7, 2026

streamFY is a movie and TV browsing app. We keep things simple: there are no accounts, no sign-up, and we do not sell your data. This page explains what is stored on your device and what is sent to other services when you use the app.

1. What we store on your device

  • Watchlist (local storage): When you tap "Watchlist", the title, poster, rating and year are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. We cannot see it, and it is never uploaded to us.
  • App cache (service worker): To load faster and work like an installed app, streamFY may save copies of its own files (pages, styles, scripts, icons) and some images in your browser's cache. This is only a copy of public app files and artwork. It does not contain personal information.

2. How the cache is used

  • The cache makes the app open faster and use less mobile data.
  • It may be updated automatically when we release a new version of the app.
  • It stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app.

3. Information we do not collect

streamFY does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location, and the app itself does not run advertising or analytics trackers. We do not create profiles about you.

4. Third-party services

To work, the app connects to other services. When it does, your device sends them standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type, and they have their own privacy policies:

  • TMDB (The Movie Database): provides titles, posters and details. Searches you type are sent to TMDB to get results. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
  • Video players / streaming embeds: when you press Watch, the video loads from an external provider inside the player. These providers may set their own cookies, show their own ads, or collect usage data. We do not control them and do not host any video files.
  • YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode): trailers are played from YouTube.
  • Google Fonts and Font Awesome (CDN): used to load fonts and icons.

5. Your choices

  • You can remove titles from your watchlist at any time from the title's page.
  • You can delete everything streamFY stored on this device with the button below, or by clearing the site's data in your browser settings.
  • You can use private/incognito mode or block third-party cookies in your browser to limit what external players store.
