Privacy Policy & Data Use

Last updated: November 12, 2026

streamFY is a movie and TV browsing app. You can browse as a guest or create an optional account, and we do not sell your data. This page explains what is stored on your device and what is sent to other services.

1. What we store on your device

  • Watchlist: when you tap Watchlist, the title, poster, rating and year are saved in your browser's local storage, under your account. We cannot see it and it is never uploaded to us. Guests cannot save a watchlist.
  • History: titles you open to watch are saved in local storage so you can find them again under History. They stay on your device.
  • Settings: your preferred streaming server and your light/dark and colour theme stay on your device.
  • App cache (service worker): copies of the app's own files and some artwork, saved so the app loads faster and uses less data. It contains no personal information.

2. Information we do not collect

If you create an account we collect your email address and a password (kept securely hashed by our sign-in provider), or your Google account's email, name and photo if you choose Continue with Google, plus the display name and avatar you pick in Settings. We do not ask for your name, phone number or location, and the app itself runs no advertising or analytics trackers. Guest mode needs no account and saves no watchlist, history or settings.

3. Third-party services

To work, the app connects to other services. When it does, your device sends them standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type. They have their own privacy policies.

  • Google Sign-In (optional): if you choose Continue with Google, Google shares your email address, name and profile photo with us through our sign-in provider. Google's own privacy policy applies to that sign-in.
  • Supabase: handles account sign-up and sign-in, and stores your email address, login session, and the display name and avatar you choose.
  • TMDB (The Movie Database): provides titles, posters and details. Searches you type are sent to TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
  • Video players and streaming embeds: when you press Watch or Open Player, the video loads from an external provider. These providers may set cookies, show ads or collect usage data. We do not control them and do not host any video files.
  • YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode): trailers are played from YouTube.
  • Google Fonts and Font Awesome (CDN): used to load fonts and icons.

4. Your choices

  • Remove a title from your watchlist at any time from its details page.
  • Delete everything streamFY stored on this device with the button below, or clear the site's data in your browser settings.
  • Use private browsing or block third-party cookies to limit what external players store.
