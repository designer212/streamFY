Privacy Policy & Data Use

Last updated: November 11, 2026

streamFY is a movie and TV browsing app. There are no accounts and no sign-up, and we do not sell your data. This page explains what is stored on your device and what is sent to other services.

1. What we store on your device

  • Watchlist: when you tap Watchlist, the title, poster, rating and year are saved in your browser's local storage. We cannot see it and it is never uploaded to us.
  • History: titles you open to watch are saved in local storage so you can find them again under History. They stay on your device.
  • Settings: your preferred streaming server stays on your device.
  • App cache (service worker): copies of the app's own files and some artwork, saved so the app loads faster and uses less data. It contains no personal information.

2. Information we do not collect

streamFY does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location, and the app itself runs no advertising or analytics trackers.

3. Third-party services

To work, the app connects to other services. When it does, your device sends them standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type. They have their own privacy policies.

  • TMDB (The Movie Database): provides titles, posters and details. Searches you type are sent to TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
  • Video players and streaming embeds: when you press Watch or Open Player, the video loads from an external provider. These providers may set cookies, show ads or collect usage data. We do not control them and do not host any video files.
  • YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode): trailers are played from YouTube.
  • Google Fonts and Font Awesome (CDN): used to load fonts and icons.

4. Your choices

  • Remove a title from your watchlist at any time from its details page.
  • Delete everything streamFY stored on this device with the button below, or clear the site's data in your browser settings.
  • Use private browsing or block third-party cookies to limit what external players store.

5. Children

streamFY is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
